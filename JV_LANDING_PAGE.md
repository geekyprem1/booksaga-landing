## 8. So Why Doesn't Everyone Publish Series?

**Headline:** So Why Isn't Everyone Publishing Series?

Because until now, writing one was a nightmare.

A series isn't five books. It's five books that must agree with each other on every detail:

- ❌ The same characters behaving the same way across hundreds of pages
- ❌ A timeline that doesn't fall apart in Book Four
- ❌ Names, ages, places and backstories tracked in a document you maintain by hand
- ❌ One big arc across the whole series, while each book still delivers on its own
- ❌ An ending in every book that makes the next one impossible to skip
- ❌ Covers that look like a family, not five strangers

Get one detail wrong (blue eyes become green, a brother becomes a cousin, the town changes its name) and your reviews fill up with readers pointing it out.

That's why most people finish one book, collapse, and never start the second.

And the alternative is worse. A ghostwriter charges four figures **per book**. For a five-book series, you're making a five-figure bet before you've earned a single cent.

## 18. Read-Through: The Number Serious Publishers Watch

**Headline:** Read-Through: The One Number Full-Time KDP Authors Obsess Over

Ask a full-time KDP author what they track. It won't be Book One sales.

It's **read-through**: the share of readers who go from Book One to Book Two, and from Two to Three.

That number decides whether ads are profitable, whether a launch is worth the effort, and whether the business grows or stalls.

Here's the catch: **a standalone book has no read-through.** There's nothing to read through to. Every sale is a dead end.

Two things drive read-through, and BookSaga builds both into every series:

1. **An ending that demands the next book.** Not a tidy wrap-up. An open question. That's what makes a reader tap "Buy" instead of closing the app.
2. **A link on the final page.** The most valuable page in your book is the one after the last chapter. Every BookSaga book ends with a "Read Next" page naming the next title, with a teaser and a link.

Get those two right and the same reader pays you again and again.

## 20. Amazon Is Built To Reward Series

**Headline:** Amazon Itself Is Built To Reward Series

Most KDP publishers never quite realise this.

Amazon doesn't just allow series. It **pushes** them.

- **📚 Series pages.** Amazon builds a dedicated page listing every book in your series, with a "Buy all" button. One click, the whole shelf.
- **🔁 Also-boughts that point back to you.** When someone buys Book One, Amazon shows them Book Two instead of a competitor's book.

Amazon's whole recommendation engine is built around readers who keep reading. Series authors ride that wave. Standalone authors paddle against it.

## 22. Kindle Unlimited: The Second Income Stream

**Headline:** And There's A Second Income Stream Most People Ignore

Kindle Unlimited changes the maths again, in your favour.

In KU, Amazon pays you **per page read**, not per sale. A subscriber who borrows your book and reads it earns you royalties, even if they'd never have paid for it.

Now picture a series inside that system.

A KU reader finishes Book One and doesn't think twice about Book Two. They borrow it instantly, because it costs them nothing extra. Then Book Three. Then Four.

**One reader can rack up thousands of page reads in a single weekend.**

That's why series dominate KU. Bingeing is free for the reader and paid for the author.

A standalone gets one borrow. A five-book series gets five, from the same person, often on the same day.
